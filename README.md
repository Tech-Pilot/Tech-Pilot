## Selected projects

### [FileNameChange](https://github.com/Tech-Pilot/FileNameChange)
**Give your PDFs names that make sense.** A native Mac app that reads document content, suggests descriptive filenames, and lets you review and revert changes. Includes on-device OCR for scanned PDFs.

<sub>Swift · SwiftUI · PDFKit · Vision</sub>

### [Spacetime Atlas](https://github.com/Tech-Pilot/Spacetime)
**Explore relativity through interaction.** Hands-on explanations of light cones, time dilation, and gravitational waves, with three levels of depth.

<sub>TypeScript · React · Interactive visualization</sub>

### [Quantum Explorer](https://github.com/Tech-Pilot/QuantumMechanics)
**Make quantum ideas easier to explore.** Interactive simulations, guided lessons, and quizzes, with three levels of explanation.

<sub>JavaScript · React · Vite · KaTeX</sub>
